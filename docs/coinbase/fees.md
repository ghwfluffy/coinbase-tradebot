### Fee Tier Calculation

Fee tiers are based on total BTC-USD spot trading volume over a rolling 30-day window.
The rolling window is recalculated every hour on the hour, and tier changes apply immediately.
Maker fees apply when liquidity is added; taker fees apply when liquidity is removed.
